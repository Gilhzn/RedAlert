# Command & Conquer: Tiberian Sun (Westwood, 1999) — Engine Mechanics Specification

**Primary sources:** retail `RULES.INI` / `FIRESTRM.INI` (Vinifera-Developers/Tiberian-Sun-INIs mirror of official game data — all numbers below are verbatim from it unless noted), ModEnc (via search snippets; site is proxy-blocked), cnc.fandom.com, CnCNet/PPM/Revora forums, StrategyWiki. Items marked **[uncertain]** could not be fully verified.

## 0. CORE SIMULATION MODEL

- **Deterministic lockstep simulation** driven by *game frames*. Nominal rate: **15 logic frames = 1 game second**; at "normal" speed 1 game second = 1 real second. Game-speed slider raises frame rate (multiplayer settings observed: 15 / 20 / 30 / 45 / 60 FPS; "fastest" uncapped). All INI values expressed in "minutes" are converted internally: **1 in-game minute = 900 frames** (60 s × 15). All rate keys (`RepairRate=.016` etc.) are minutes → frames.
- **Distance unit: the lepton. 1 cell = 256×256 leptons.** Vertical (height) leptons are halved relative to horizontal because the isometric cell art is half as tall as wide; **128 leptons ≈ 1 terrain elevation level**. On screen a cell is a 48×24-pixel diamond (isometric dimetric projection); one height level shifts drawing up 12 px. Multiple height levels (cliffs, ramps); bridges span at elevated height (jumpjet `CruiseHeight=500` is documented in rules.ini as "higher than a bridge, just to be safe").
- **Infantry sub-cells**: multiple infantry share one cell at sub-cell offsets (TS/RA2 engine: 3 usable sub-positions per cell) **[uncertain — TD/RA used 5-per-cell "dice" pattern; TS is widely cited as 3]**.
- Weapon `Speed`/unit `Speed` values are movement rates in leptons/frame scale (projectile `Speed=100` = fast/hitscan-ish; unit Speed 3–25 range, modified by terrain % tables below) **[exact leptons-per-frame conversion uncertain]**.
- `GameSpeedBias=1` global movement multiplier.

## 1. ECONOMY

### Tiberium types (`[Tiberiums]`: Riparius, Cruentus, Vinifera, Aboreus)
| Type | Value/bail | Power (explosiveness)/bail | Growth% per pass | Spread% | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Riparius** (green, Image=1) | **25** | 4 | 0.09%/frame-check, interval 2200 | 0.09 / 2200 | grows & spreads fast; not meaningfully explosive |
| **Cruentus** (blue crystal, Image=2) | 70 | 10 | 0 (never) | 0 | unused impassable crystal form; drops CRYSTAL debris |
| **Vinifera** (blue, Image=3) | **40** | **100** | 0.05 / 10000 | 0.05 / 10000 | slow growth, **highly explosive** |
| Aboreus (Image=4) | 30 | 10 | 0.05 / 10000 | same | duplicate blue variant |

- **Chain reactions**: `TiberiumExplosive=yes`; `TiberiumExplosionDamage=100` per exploding cell; `TiberiumStrength=20` (resistance to detonating); blue fields chain-react cell-to-cell, flinging `Shard` crystals; harvesters full of blue explode violently (`Explodes=yes` on HARV).
- **Per-cell state**: tiberium is an overlay (TIB01.., TIB2_.., TIB3_.. sets) with a density/growth level per cell; internally cells with level < 11 are eligible to grow denser, cells with level > 6 can seed adjacent cells (12 density stages, 0–11). Global switches `TiberiumGrows=yes`, `TiberiumSpreads=yes`, master timer `GrowthRate=5` minutes between growth passes. Trees of type `SpawnsTiberium=yes` (blossom trees) seed tiberium around them.
- **Tiberium vs infantry**: walking on tiberium damages any infantry without `TiberiumProof=yes`; infantry killed by tiberium/tiberium gas can transform into a visceroid — `TiberiumTransmogrify=40` (percent chance) **[semantics: % chance, uncertain]**; `SmallVisceroid=VISC_SML`, two small merge → `LargeVisceroid=VISC_LRG`. Units with `TiberiumHeal=yes` (Cyborg, Cyborg Commando, Umagon, Ghost Stalker, fiends, visceroids) heal standing in tiberium, tick every `TiberiumHeal=.010` min (9 frames).
- **Harvester** (HARV): `Storage=28` bails, `Dock=PROC`, heavy armor, 1000 HP, cost 1400, Speed 5, `Weight=1` (light enough not to crack ice!), crewed, explodes when destroyed. Full green load = 28×25 = **700 credits**; full blue = 28×40 = **1120**. AI-side auto behavior: scans `TiberiumNearScan=6` cells for more tiberium in current patch, `TiberiumFarScan=48` cells for a new patch; auto-replaced by the computer at IQ≥2 (`[IQ] Harvester=2`). Harvest animation docks/unloads using separate art (`UnloadingHarvester=HORV`). Refinery has one dock cell; harvesters queue. `HarvesterImmune=no` option; `HarvesterUnit=HARV`; each refinery spawns a `FreeUnit=HARV`.
- **Refinery** (PROC): cost 2000, 900 HP, Power −30, **Storage=80 bails**, `DockUnload=yes`, `Bib=yes`. **Silo** (GASILO): cost 150, 300 HP, Power −10, **Storage=60 bails**. Storage is measured in bails; when total house storage is full, further unloading is lost (EVA "silos needed") **[overflow-discard behavior standard for the series; uncertain whether credited-then-capped]**. Credits are stored as bails × value; selling/losing silos with stored tiberium loses that storage capacity.
- **Weed / veins economy (Nod)**: `Weeder=yes` **Weed Eater** (WEED, cost 1400, Storage=7 weed units, `Dock=NAWAST`, spawned free by the **Tiberium Waste Facility** NAWAST, BuildLimit=1, cost 1600). House must accumulate **`WeedCapacity=56`** weed to arm one **chemical missile**. Veins (overlay `VEINS`) are produced by **veinhole monsters** (see §6). Weeds land type is harvestable only by weeders.
- **Income/production globals**: `BuildSpeed=.8` (minutes per 1000 credits of cost), `BuildupTime=.06` (construction anim), `RefundPercent=50%` on sale, `SurvivorRate=.4` of building cost converted to escaping infantry (÷ `SurvivorDivisor=100` per soldier; Firestorm lowers rate to .1), `CrewEscape=50%` chance vehicle crew bails, `MultipleFactory=0` (**no** speed bonus for extra factories in TS), `MaximumQueuedObjects=4` (queue up to 4 waiting + 1 building = 5 per production tab; community docs cite "queue of 5"), `MaxMoney` default 10000 start in MP (`Money=10000`).

## 2. CONSTRUCTION & BASE

- **Sidebar**: two side-by-side strips — left column: structures (from ConYard `Factory=BuildingType`); right column: units. Separate concurrent production queues per factory class: **BuildingType, InfantryType, UnitType (vehicles), AircraftType** (helipad is the aircraft factory; `SeparateAircraft=yes` = first aircraft bought separately from helipad). One item builds at a time per class; clicking again queues (max above). Completed structures wait for manual placement; `PlacementDelay=.05` retry delay. Units exit via factory `ExitCell`; rally points: Ctrl+Alt+click.
- **Placement / adjacency**: buildings must touch the footprint-proximity radius of an existing `BaseNormal=yes` building of your house: per-building `Adjacent=` distance in cells beyond footprint (ConYard/factories 2; walls & gates 4; component tower 3; defenses like NALASR/NASAM 4; deployed tick tanks/artillery/sensor arrays have `BaseNormal=no` so they do NOT extend the base). Terrain must be `Buildable` land (Clear, Road, Rough) and same height level, flattened; bibs occupy extra rows.
- **Power**: house power = Σ positive `Power=` − Σ drains. **Low power** (drain > output): radar off (`Powered=true` buildings shut down — radar, SAMs, Obelisk, lasers get disabled if `Powered=yes`: GARADR/NARADR, GACSAM, NASAM, NAOBEL, NAPOST laser fence, NASTLH stealth gen, GAPLUG), build speed scales between `WorstLowPowerBuildRateCoefficient=.3` and `BestLowPowerBuildRateCoefficient=.75` (general floor `MinProductionSpeed=.5`); superweapon timers governed by `IsPowered=true` pause. GDI Power Plant 300cr/+100, upgradable with 2 × **Power Turbine** (100cr, +50 each); Nod Power Plant 300cr/+100; Nod **Advanced Power Plant** 500cr/+200 (needs War Factory). AI sells buildings if power < `PowerEmergency=75%`. `TogglePower` allows manual on/off of drains (ConYard `TogglePower=no`).
- **Build-speed formula**: time ≈ (cost/1000) × `BuildSpeed` min × difficulty `BuildTime` × power penalty; walls use `WallBuildSpeedCoefficient=.5` (2× faster per cost).
- **Repair / sell**: building repair costs `RepairPercent=20%` of full cost pro-rata, applies `RepairStep=8` HP per tick every `RepairRate=.016` min (~1/sec); units at Service Depot (GADEPT, `UnitRepair=yes`, `UnitReload=yes`) use `URepairRate=.016`; infantry `IRepairRate=.001`/`IRepairStep=1` (hospital). Selling refunds 50%, spawns survivors; `SellSound`, sell of walls not possible (`Insignificant`, unselectable). Mobile Repair Vehicle (Nod REPAIR, 1000cr) heals vehicles in field with `RepairBullet` (Damage −50, Mechanical warhead, ROF 80). GDI **Drop pods** & repair: N/A.
- **Walls & fences**: Concrete Wall GAWALL / Nod Wall NAWALL: 50cr, 150 HP, `Armor=concrete`, `Wall=yes`, block ground movement and direct-fire LOS for most ballistic weapons; only warheads with `Wall=yes` damage them (`Wood=yes` for wooden obstacles). Adjacent=4 allows building wall lines away from base. Component Tower (GACTWR, 200cr, 500 HP) is GDI's plug-in defense mount, upgrades: **Vulcan** (150cr, SA 18dmg/ROF26/rng6), **RPG** (600cr, 110dmg/ROF80/rng8, MinimumRange=2), **SAM** (300cr, RedEye2 AA 33dmg/ROF55/rng15, `Powered`); tower connects wall segments (`WallTower=GACTWR`).
- **Laser fence (Nod)**: Laser Fence Post NAPOST (200cr, 300 HP, Power −25, `LaserFencePost=yes`, `Powered=yes`) — posts auto-project NAFNCE fence sections (800 HP, unselectable, `LaserFence=yes`) between posts in range; sections impassable while powered; **power loss drops the beams** (passable) — classic weakness.
- **Firestorm defense (GDI)**: Firestorm Generator GAFIRE (2000cr, 800 HP, Power −200) + Firestorm Wall Sections GAFSDF (50cr each, 200 HP, Power −2 each, `FirestormWall=yes`). Superweapon `FirestormSpecial`, `RechargeTime=3` min to full charge, `UseChargeDrain=true`: player toggles wall on; while active the energy field is **impassable to everything and destroys what touches it** — ground units entering die, projectiles/missiles are annihilated (some projectiles set `IgnoresFirestorm=yes`: multi-missile, chem missile, Banshee proton — i.e. those pass over in MP; fandom notes SP missions can block SW missiles), aircraft flying over are destroyed. Charge drains while on at 3× charge rate (`ChargeToDrainRatio=.333`); weapon damage hitting active sections drains charge via `DamageToFirestormDamageCoefficient=.1`; `FirestormWarhead=FirestormWH` (100% vs all armor, InfDeath=4) kills occupants. EMP and Hunter-Seeker impact can knock the system offline.
- **Gates**: GAGATE_A/B, NAGATE_A/B (250cr, 350 HP, heavy, `Gate=yes`, E-W and N-S variants): auto-open for friendly ground units, stay closed to enemies; `GateCloseDelay` (default hardcoded ~ a couple of game seconds **[uncertain]**); repairable, not capturable. AI uses `NSGates`/`EWGates` when walling (`AIBuildsWalls=no`, `NodAIBuildsWalls=no` by default).
- **MCV / ConYard**: MCV (2500cr, 1000 HP, heavy, Speed 3, `DeploysInto=GACNST`) requires 2×2 flat buildable area; ConYard `UndeploysInto=MCV` — **TS supports re-packing the ConYard** (multiplayer option "MCV redeploys"; in campaign generally allowed) **[option-gating in original MP lobby uncertain]**. Crate `FreeMCV=yes` can grant an MCV if the player has no buildings but has money. `BaseUnit=MCV` ("home" when no buildings).
- **Engineers & capture**: Engineer (500cr, 100 HP, `Engineer=yes`) enters `Capturable=true` enemy building → **instant capture (100→full ownership) in base TS**; entering a friendly damaged building restores it to full HP (consumes engineer); also repairs bridges via bridge huts and captures neutral tech buildings. Patch 2.03 added the optional **"Multi Engineer"** MP mode (RA-style): keys `EngineerCaptureLevel=1.0`, `EngineerDamage=0.0` (Firestorm rules) — with multi-engineer on, each engineer removes a chunk of HP and capture only occurs below a health threshold **[exact threshold values used by the 2.03 toggle uncertain]**. Capturing enemy production grants that side's tech tree from that factory ("tech stealing": e.g., capture Hand of Nod → build Nod infantry). ConYards are capturable; capture of a ConYard while placement cursor active was a 1.13 crash fix. `Capturable=false` on defenses, gates, silos? (silos are capturable=true), walls etc.
- **Prerequisite classes** (`[General]`): `PrerequisitePower=GAPOWR,NAPOWR,NAAPWR`, `PrerequisiteFactory=GAWEAP,NAWEAP`, `PrerequisiteBarracks=NAHAND,GAPILE`, `PrerequisiteRadar=GARADR,NARADR`, `PrerequisiteTech=GATECH,NATECH`. TechLevel 1–10 gates campaign progression; `TechLevel=-1` = never player-buildable.
- **Key build-tree costs** (HP/cost/power): ConYard 1000/2500/0; Power 750/300/+100; Barracks 800/300/−20; Refinery 900/2000/−30; War Factory 1000/2000/−30; Radar 1000/1000/−40; Helipad 600/500/−10 (`Factory=AircraftType`, `UnitReload`); Service Depot 1100/1200/−30; Tech Center 500/1500/−200(GDI)/−100(Nod); GDI Upgrade Center GAPLUG 1000/1000/−150 with plugs: Threat Rating Node (500, unused AI aid), **Seeker Control** (1000/−50, grants Hunter-Seeker), **Ion Cannon Uplink** (1500/−100, grants Ion Cannon); Temple of Nod 1000/2000/−200 (grants Hunter-Seeker for Nod, target of `HSBuilding`); Missile Silo NAMISL 1000/1300/−50 (Multi-missile); Waste Facility 400/1600/−40; Stealth Generator NASTLH 600/2500/−350 `CloakGenerator=yes CloakRadiusInCells=12`; EMP Cannon NAPULS 500/1000/−150 (**Owner=Nod,GDI — both sides**, `Prerequisite=Radar`); Obelisk 725/1500/−150; Nod Laser 500/300/−40; SAM 600/500/−30.

- **Slopes**: `TrackedUphill=.5`, `TrackedDownhill=1.1`, `WheeledUphill=.5`, `WheeledDownhill=1.2` speed coefficients. Height advantage: `LeptonsPerSightIncrease=2000`, `LeptonsPerFireIncrease=2000` (higher units see/fire farther). `CliffBackImpassability=2` (cells directly behind cliff faces maximally impassable). Vehicles `IsTilter=yes` visually tilt on slopes.
- **Locomotors** (CLSID → behavior): `…41` Drive (tracked/wheeled ground), `…42` Hover (Hover MLRS, APC? — HVR uses it; `SpeedType=Hover`), `…43` **Tunnel/subterranean** (Devil's Tongue, Sub APC — travel underground between dig points; `TunnelSpeed=1`, `AllowShroudedSubteranneanMoves=true`, dig anim `Dig`/`DigSound`; cannot surface on pavement/buildings; while underground untargetable except detection by sensor arrays), `…44` Walk (infantry), `…45` Drop pod, `…46` Fly (aircraft), `{55D141B8…}` **Walker/Mech** (Titan MMCH, Wolverine SMECH, Mammoth Mk. II HMEC — walking gait, crushing), `{92612C46…}` **Jumpjet** (Jumpjet Infantry: `[JumpjetControls] TurnRate=4 Speed=14 Climb=5 CruiseHeight=500 Acceleration=2 WobblesPerSecond=.15 WobbleDeviation=40`; flies over cliffs/water, lands to fire? no — fires airborne with JumpCannon AA/AG).
- **Hover mechanics**: `HoverHeight=120` leptons, `HoverDampen=40%`, `HoverBob=.04`, `HoverBoost=150%` speed on straightaways, `HoverAcceleration=.02`, `HoverBrake=.03`. Hover units pass water; immobilized during ion storms; ignore veins (vein immunity list: Wolverines, hover craft, harvesters, weeders).
- **MovementZone** classes (pathfinding domains): Normal, Infantry, InfantryDestroyer, Destroyer, Crusher, AmphibiousCrusher, AmphibiousDestroyer, Subterannean [sic], Fly.
- **Facing/turning**: 8/16/32-facing sprites & voxels; `ROT=` per unit (body and turret), e.g. tanks 5, bikes 8, visceroids 16, HMEC 3. Projectile `ROT` for homing missiles (HeatSeeker 8, SAM 5).
- **Terrain deformation**: explosions deform the height mesh — warheads carry `Deform=%` chance and `DeformThreshhold=` (min damage), e.g. HE 10%/300, ARTYHE 15%/120, IonCannonWH `Deform=100%`; meteors crater via `CraterLevel=1` (0–4). `CollapseChance=100` % chance a cliff hit collapses. Craters/scorches are smudge overlays.
- **Bridges**: `DestroyableBridges=yes`, `BridgeStrength=1500` HP per section; destroy by damage; **repair via bridge repair huts** (CABHUT, immune, engineer enters hut to rebuild span); units under high bridges pass beneath (z-buffer fudges: ZFudgeBridge etc.); `BridgeVoxelMax=3` debris; TooBigToFitUnderBridge=true (Hover MLRS) blocked beneath.
- **Ice**: crackable/breakable by weight: `IceCrackingWeight=2.0`, `IceBreakingWeight=4.0` (most combat vehicles Weight=3.5 → crack; heavies >4 → fall through; harvester Weight=1 safe); cracked ice re-solidifies after `IceSolidifyFrameTime=1000` frames; `IceGrowthRate=1.5`; ships/none in TS; infantry safe (Foot 50%).
- **Veins damage**: vehicles on veins take `VeinDamage=5` per interval (VeinholeWH, `Veinhole=yes` warhead); infantry immune (except jumpjet per lore); `ImmuneToVeins=yes` on weeder/harvester; `VeinAttack=VEINATAC` anim.
- **Pathfinding**: A*-based hierarchical pathfinder (Ars Technica "War Stories" interview); `CloseEnough=2.25` cells abort threshold; `PathDelay=.01` min retry when blocked; `BlockagePathDelay=60` frames before repathing around blockage; `Stray=2.0` team cohesion radius; `MaxWaypointPathLength=15`.
